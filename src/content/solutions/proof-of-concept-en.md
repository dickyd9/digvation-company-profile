title: Proof of Concept
eyebrow: Solution 02
summary: For ideas with enough technical or business uncertainty that a full build would be premature.
problem: 'A PoC answers the risky questions first: is the approach feasible, is the data sufficient, do the devices work in the environment, or does the workflow improve in practice?'
when:
  - AI or computer vision has not been tested in real conditions
  - Integration depends on third-party systems
  - IoT or devices need field validation
whatWeDo:
  - Define the uncertainty
  - Build the smallest useful demonstration
  - Test against real constraints
  - Document findings and next-step recommendation
outputs:
  - Working demonstration
  - Feasibility findings
  - Risk and limitation notes
  - Recommendation for production or stop
examples:
  - Camera analytics in a specific environment
  - OCR across varied document formats
  - Sensors or devices with field connectivity
start: Start with the riskiest question, not a full feature list.
