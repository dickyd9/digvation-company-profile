# Full Experience Redesign Audit

## Preserved

- Astro static architecture and Cloudflare Pages deployment model.
- Indonesian and English routing.
- Typed Content Collections.
- Inquiry Pages Function, validation, honeypot, Turnstile, Resend, and webhook options.
- Consent-aware GA4, GTM, and Clarity foundation.
- Session campaign attribution.
- Canonical, hreflang, sitemap, robots, OG/Twitter, JSON-LD, and legal pages.
- Astro route transitions and content-first progressive enhancement.

## Changed

- Homepage narrative now moves from a minimal proposition directly into real work.
- Real screenshots replace illustrative HTML/CSS dashboards.
- Work listing and two bilingual case studies use a product-story format.
- Solutions use a one-screen closed directory with inline detail.
- Process uses six understandable stages and real interface evidence.
- About focuses on purpose, belief, and direction rather than a service list.
- Contact uses a stronger inquiry composition and centralized conversion paths.
- Analytics events now match business intent and avoid duplicate success events.
- Work content schema now supports visibility, order, feature status, richer evidence, and optional results.
- Pricing and campaign systems are configurable without publishing invented commercial content.
- The corrective homepage is reduced to five moments: proposition, real proof, business actions, process, and final conversation.
- One Inter variable family replaces the two-family typography setup.
- Repeated fade-up animation is replaced by a tiered system of masks, line wipes, directional reveals, project expansion, and restrained micro-interactions.

## Removed after reference verification

- CSS-built dashboard, problem, and solution scenes.
- Homepage slider controls and slider-specific sections.
- Handwritten-note system and Caveat font.
- Live iframe preview and preview modal.
- Generic project fallback visual.
- Fourteen localized solution-detail routes, replaced by redirects.
- Homepage client-logo and FAQ sections that competed with stronger project proof.

## External validation boundary

The local build validates generated pages and assets. Real inquiry delivery, provider analytics, Turnstile hostname enforcement, Cloudflare response headers, rate limiting, and externally observable security behavior require an authorized staging or production target with credentials.
