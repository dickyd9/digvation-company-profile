# Content Strategy

For the current sales-focused version, Digvation does not maintain a public Insights/blog section. Organic growth should first come from high-quality pages that directly support commercial intent and real evidence.

## Priority content surfaces

1. Solution pages for real business problems.
2. Case studies for approved client work.
3. Service/problem FAQs based on real sales questions.
4. Clear work pages that can earn client/partner backlinks.
5. Accurate local/business information when applicable.

## Potential future educational content

A blog or insight library can be introduced later when Digvation has a consistent publishing cadence and a clear keyword/content strategy. It should not exist merely to make the website look bigger.
